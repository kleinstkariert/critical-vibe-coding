### llm provider favoritism
- microsoft azure has a ~33% market share but in testing only 2% of examples used it (Zhang et al., 2025)
- Google can potentially establish a digital monopoly by leveraging its LLM to preferentially promote its own services in code recommendations.(Zhang et al., 2025)
- Zhang (2025) concludes: “It can not only foster unfair competition in the digital market but also undermine user autonomy, disrupting the digital ecosystem and even societal order”

### AI Deskilling Is a Structural Problem

---

### Deskilling means losing a capacity because we hand it over to an external tool.

---

### deskilling

- Deskilling is always part of human history.
- But is not only losing skills like coding or navigating.
-  It is losing the “arts of personhood”: epistemic, social, moral and creative capacities.
- The problem is the environment, not weak individuals.

---

### LLMs as artificial personal assistants (APAs)

- LLMs are used as personal assistants for planning, values, and life advice.
- They are always available, adaptive, and non-judgmental.
- Overreliance on APAs could lead to a “capacity-hostile environment” that allows only shallow skill development.
- Users become plan-followers, not plan-formers.
- Less human interaction means fewer chances to build capacities together.

---

There is growing evidence of the erosion of critical thinking, decision-making and analytical reasoning as a result of overreliance on artificial intelligence (AI).
