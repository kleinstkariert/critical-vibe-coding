No NVLink on the RTX 4090. Each card needs adequate native power delivery: about six separate 8-pin leads in total, or two native 12V-2x6 connections. Seat the plug fully. Do not bend the cable sharply near the plug. Do not use unapproved extensions.

Wall draw: about 1.0–1.2 kW (*estimate, no source recorded*). Shared cautions are in the [README](README.md#procurement-and-operation-cautions).
