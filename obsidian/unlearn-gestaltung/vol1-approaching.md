# Volume 1 — Approaching AI in Aesthetic Practices

**Source:** PDF https://unlearn.gestaltung.ai/un_learn_ai-1-sm.pdf · site https://unlearn.gestaltung.ai/ · DOI [10.25358/openscience-10586](https://doi.org/10.25358/openscience-10586)  
**Editors (Vol. 1):** Julia-Jasmin Bold, Maika Dieterich  
**Project:** KITeGG (HS Mainz lead; partners Offenbach, Schwäbisch Gmünd, KISD/TH Köln, Trier)  
**Covered period (per front matter):** project start Winter 2021/22 through Summer 2023

## Main points

1. **Series premise.** First of three books documenting a four-year BMBF/RLP-funded Verbund: make AI *greifbar und begreifbar* in Gestaltung teaching — technology + society through design.
2. **“Approaching” = orientation.** Build shared vocabulary, labs, first courses, Summer/Winter schools; start the central Lehr-Lernplattform (LLP); map questions rather than lock a single pedagogy.
3. **Hybrid publishing is part of the research.** PubPub + Paged.js web-to-print; print short-IDs map to online articles; **semantic chunking** links related passages across volumes for “horizontal” reading (and chunk-chain PDFs).
4. **Institutional labs as curriculum infrastructure.** Volume opens with Standort portraits and dedicated lab interviews (Living Objects Lab, autoLab, KINDLAB, Robotik Lab, KI Labor, AI+D Lab) — physical + thematic spaces for forschendes Lernen, not only software installs.
5. **Teaching + student work + research mix.** TOC clusters: lab Blick → Lehre (latent space, robotics/CV, creativity/collaboration, Touch×AI, type/image gen, teaching platform, course list) → student projects (Stable Diffusion aesthetics, Type Table, DeadHappy, Street Porcelain, etc.) → research/symposia threads.
6. **Design-education focus.** Emphasis on Gestaltung curricula, prototyping, generative image/type, physical computing — not agent harnesses or personal knowledge wikis as the default stack.

## Differences from our course

See also [[comparison-vs-cvc]].

- **Scale & form:** multi-year Verbund + print trilogy + shared cluster/labs vs one-semester personal-agent seminar (Hermes + Obsidian / LLM Wiki).
- **Object of practice:** aesthetic / design / physical-AI studios first; CVC Session 01 frames **model → product → agent** and political economy of LLM platforms.
- **Unlearn framing:** what designers must *unlearn* to engage AI critically/imaginatively (Editor’s Note across volumes) — CVC uses twin tracks (tech + critical) but does not brand “unlearning” as the method.
- **Publishing as system:** semantic chunk navigation and web-to-print pipeline are first-class research artefacts; CVC course GitHub/Obsidian is teaching infra, not a designed publication network.
