# Volume 3 — Integrating AI in Aesthetic Practices

**Source:** PDF https://unlearn.gestaltung.ai/un_learn_ai-3-sm.pdf · DOI [10.25358/openscience-13020](https://doi.org/10.25358/openscience-13020)  
**Editors (Vol. 3):** Matthias Grund, Jana Hartmann, Jakob Kilian, Leon-Etienne Kühr, Mattis Kuhn, Johanna T. Wallenborn, Lasse Scherffig  
**Editor’s Note:** https://unlearn.gestaltung.ai/article/cetyu72y

## Main points

1. **“Integrating” = arrival + handoff.** Courses travel across partner schools; inter-university formats (*KI-Abend*, *Making (Non)Sense*, *Thinking and Prompting: Images*); reflection on what structures stuck.
2. **Unlearn as method.** Goal was never only technical proficiency — what must be *unlearned* for critical, ethical, imaginative engagement (Editor’s Note).
3. **Three book parts (Vol. 3 structure).** Educational strategies · student project showcase (nonlinear/visual) · experimental research treating AI as discursive / affective / material, not only tool.
4. **Institutional outcome beyond the books.** Inter-institutional MA strand *Design is All You Need – AI and Design Education* inside Communication Design M.A. at HS Mainz, folding partner formats into an ongoing program.
5. **Infrastructure articles land here.** Koch’s *Der KITeGG Cluster*, *How to KITeGG*, *DIY Compute*; literacy pillars (Kilian; Flechtner/Tost); Critical AI Studies intro; Physical AI teaching challenges — i.e. the series closes by documenting **how the shared systems were built**, not only studio results.
6. **Open questions kept urgent.** Aesthetics of deep learning; training-data assumptions; when/why/**when not** to use systems; empower designers to shape, not only consume AI.
7. **Hybrid publishing reflexivity.** Short-IDs + semantic chunks across all three volumes; series as document *and* network.

## Differences from our course

- **Programmatic scale:** Verbund → shared MA pathway; CVC deliverable is **personal agent architecture + one use + reflection**, not a multi-school degree redesign.
- **“When not to use AI”** appears as a closing research question in Vol. 3; CVC schedules a dedicated Session 11 (*ecology / when not*) earlier in a 13-week map — similar ethic, different packaging.
- **Research-as-art / alternative research formats** are a named third of Vol. 3; CVC critical track is weekly but less publication-oriented.
- **Striking:** Vol. 3 treats **reproducible institutional platform engineering** (Kubernetes cluster case study) as aesthetic-education research; CVC treats **harness + wiki** as the student-facing architecture.
