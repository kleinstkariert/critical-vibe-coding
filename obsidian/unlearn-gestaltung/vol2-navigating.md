# Volume 2 — Navigating AI in Aesthetic Practices

**Source:** PDF https://unlearn.gestaltung.ai/un_learn_ai-2-sm.pdf · DOI [10.25358/openscience-11811](https://doi.org/10.25358/openscience-11811)  
**Editors (Vol. 2):** Florian Jenett, Rahel Flechtner, Simon Maris, Jordi Tost, Julia-Jasmin Bold  
**Keywords (cover strip):** kitegg · physical ai · generative ai · research · symposia · design & art education · society · practice-based labs

## Main points

1. **“Navigating” = experimentation after orientation.** Test course formats, discursive spaces, teaching methods once generative models (ChatGPT, Stable Diffusion, multimodal agents) hit public reach (Editor’s Note / series frame).
2. **Physical AI as a major axis.** Editorial and teaching threads on Physical AI, robotics, controllers, embodied / nonsensical making — AI not only as image/text generator but as material, kinetic, and sensor practice.
3. **Human–AI partnership editorial line.** Explicit curriculum negotiation: theory ↔ practice, teaching ↔ research, institutions ↔ individuals; partnership framed as navigational, not “tool tips.”
4. **Society / critical research densifies.** Stronger editorial and research presence on AI & society, algorithmic gaze, ownership/control of generative models, gender/politics of AI imagery, sustainability of “neutral” AI rhetoric (site TOC + Vol. 3 continuations).
5. **Cluster as shared teaching substrate.** Cross-references to the shared infrastructure (Jupyter/services/LLP) appear alongside site labs — navigation happens on **common compute**, not only on student laptops.
6. **PDF note:** Vol. 2 “sm” PDF text layer is sparse/garbled in places (design-heavy pages); condensation leans on site catalog + Editor’s Note + linked articles, not OCR of every essay.

## Differences from our course

- **Physical AI / robotics labs** are central to Vol. 2 pedagogy; CVC mentions CAD/3D/MCP and fab later, but default stack is chat-agent + wiki, not robot/sensor studios.
- **Shared institutional GPU services** assumed; CVC Session 04 is optional personal/local hardware with “no one must buy.”
- **Multi-site co-teaching and Summer/Winter schools** as the rhythm; CVC uses weekly 120-min twin tracks + student humanities pairs.
