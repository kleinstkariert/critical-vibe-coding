## Project in one line

**KITeGG** = four-year (~2021–2025) multi-university design-education research project: shared **bare-metal GPU cluster**, site **AI labs**, courses, Summer/Winter schools, and the three *un/learn ai* books — oriented to Gestaltung / aesthetic practice, not a single personal-agent seminar.
