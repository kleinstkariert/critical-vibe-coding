# Session 01 — Welcome outdoors + political-economic lecture

Parent: [[../timeline]] · Outline: [[../course-outline]]

## Goals

- Open a shared space for fears, hopes, and current workflows (technical course + “group therapy”)
- Map what students already use (agents, knowledge bases, subscriptions)
- Give a first technical + political-economic frame for LLMs and agents
- Propose the default personal stack: Hermes + Obsidian + LLM Wiki (+ individual model/plan)

## Part A — Intro/ Opener check-in (40–60 min)


**Standing course check-in** (also used indoors from session 2 — listen only, 30–60 s/person; full rules [[../course-rhythm]]):

1. How was your morning?  
2. What did you do with AI since we last met, and how was that experience?  

For session 1, expand into the longer outdoor prompts below (still listen-first):

### How it feels

- How do you feel about AI in your daily / artistic workflow right now?
- What are you afraid of (skill loss, sameness, surveillance, dependency, job pressure, greenwashing…)?
- What do you hope for (speed, access, a co-reader, new forms…)?

### What you already use

- Which tools are in your current workflow (editors, chatbots, node tools, CAD, fab…)?
- Are you already using **agents** (tool-using systems), or only chat windows?
- Do you keep a **personal knowledge base**? Where (notes app, folder, Notion, Obsidian…)?
- Has anyone heard of / used **Obsidian**? Git? Local models?
- Do you know the term "lint" ?

### Politics, economy, labour (open conversation)

- Why do these systems exist *now* — who pays, who profits, who is sold as the user?
- What do you think LLMs do to **job markets** in art, design, code, education?
- What **political** uses worry or interest you (persuasion, bureaucracy, surveillance, activism)?
- Where do you notice hype vs. something you can actually use?

### Quick inventory (can be show of hands)

- Who has coded before, and with which tools?
- Who has a paid LLM subscription (which)?
- Who has used MCP, Cursor, Claude Code, Hermes, OpenClaw, or similar?

Capture themes on paper; bring them into the lecture room.

## Part B — Classroom input lecture

**Full first-draft speaker notes + image suggestions:** [[../lectures/01-welcome-landscape-lecture]]

Presentation style: [[../presentation-style]]

Short Q&A after each block. Priority airtime: **#2 (technical)**, **#3 (visions/fears — 10–15 min)**, and **#4 (bubble / economy)**.

1. **Scope of the class** through the semester (twin tracks: powerful personal agent + critical frame; agent as interface).
2. **What LLMs and agent systems are** (technically): models vs. chat products vs. agents with tools/skills/files — layers, tokens/context, hallucination, local vs cloud preview.
3. **Drivers, visions, fears, regulation** (10–15 min talk) — growth treadmill + data/compute; utopia (UBI/UHI) vs x-risk double message; amplifier vs power; what law can do. Handout: [[../visions-fears-regulation]]. Law detail later: [[../eu-ai-act-and-datenschutz]]. Labour deep dive: Session [[10-authorship-labour-platforms]].
4. **Economic background / potential AI bubble** — three boxes (financial / productive / dead end); BIS circularity; Tooze skeptical PE; Acemoglu modest productivity. Deep research: [[../../research/ai-bubble-productivity-datacenter]]. Teacher clippings (vault, not student kit): Tooze Ep. 250/260, Acemoglu MIT Sloan, Verdegem commons under `Clippings/`.
5. **Ecological and social implications** — energy, water, where data centers sit (seed only); job-market claims vs observed shifts. **Deep dive:** Session 11 + [[../../research/data-centers-energy-ecology-economy]] (cross-ref bubble CapEx: [[../../research/ai-bubble-productivity-datacenter]]).
6. **What this might mean for media-art workflows** (teacher’s view): authorship, speed, taste flattening, new interfaces, productivity, democratization, when *not* to use agents.
7. **Suggested default setup (preview only):** Hermes + Obsidian + LLM Wiki. Architecture next week — [[02-agent-harness-and-wiki-architecture]] · [[../what-is-harness]]. How-tos: [[../howto-obsidian-setup-and-sync]], [[../howto-hermes-setup]]. Student kit (not whole vault): [[../student-share-structure]].
