# Session 08. Final presentations and closing check-in

Parent: [[../timeline]] · Rhythm: [[../course-rhythm]]

- Present the personal agent architecture + one artistic or research use + a short critical reflection
- Close the group-therapy loop: hopes and fears revisited
- Collect what should change next semester

## Final presentation (suggested structure)

About 5 to 8 min + feedback (about 20 students; flag if the group is larger):

1. **Agent architecture:** diagram or live walkthrough; Session 02 harness + Session 05 tools + `SCHEMA.md` ([[../llm-wiki-standard-files]] · [[../what-is-harness]])
2. **Use:** one artistic or research scenario where the *setup* matters (Session 06 interface score)
3. **Critical reflection:** platform, ecology, authorship, labour, regulation, bubble, flattening, privacy; cite a primary source (Session 01 deep dives, the student inputs)
4. **Provenance note:** which model, which tools, who decided (how), where it ran
5. **Open question** you still hold

Not a polished exhibition by default. Students who chose research instead of AI use present their investigation.

## Closing round

- Session 01 fears and hopes: what still stands?
- What will you keep running after the course?
- Feedback on check-in, twin tracks, student inputs, agent as interface
