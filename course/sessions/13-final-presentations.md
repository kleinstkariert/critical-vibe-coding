# Session 13 — Final presentations + closing check-in

Parent: [[../timeline]]

## Goals

- Present personal agent architecture + artistic/research use + short critical reflection
- Close the “group therapy” loop: hopes/fears revisited
- Collect what should change next semester

## Check-in (opening)

One sentence: what shifted since the outdoor first session?

## Final presentation (suggested structure)

~10–15 min + feedback:

1. **Agent architecture** — diagram or live walkthrough (model, vault, skills, tools, phone/Discord); session 2 harness diagram updated with session 5 tools, plus `SCHEMA.md` ([[../llm-wiki-standard-files]] · [[../what-is-harness]])
2. **Use** — one artistic or research scenario where the *setup* matters
3. **Critical reflection** — pick at least one: platform dependency, ecology, authorship, labour, regulation hype, bubble; cite at least one primary source (the session 9 / 10 reading tables are the starting point)
4. **Open question** you still hold

Not a polished exhibition by default (*technical suggestion* in [[../timeline]]).

*technical suggestion:* the `log.md` of each wiki is a ready-made record of the semester (ingests, queries, lint passes) — one slide of it says more about the practice than a feature list.

## Closing round

- Return to session 1 fears/hopes — what still stands?
- What will you keep running after the course?
- Feedback for the seminar format (outdoor check-in, twin tracks, agent-as-interface)
