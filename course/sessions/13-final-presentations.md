## Final presentation (suggested structure)

~10-15 min + feedback:

1. **Agent architecture** - diagram or live walkthrough; session 2 harness + session 6 tools + `SCHEMA.md` ([[../llm-wiki-standard-files]] ? [[../what-is-harness]])
2. **Use** - one artistic or research scenario where the *setup* matters
3. **Critical reflection** - platform, ecology, authorship, labour, regulation, bubble; cite a primary source ([[10-authorship-labour-platforms]] / [[11-local-ecology-when-not]])
4. **Open question** you still hold

Not a polished exhibition by default.
