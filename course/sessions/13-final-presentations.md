# Session 13 ? Final presentations + closing check-in

Parent: [[../timeline]] ? Rhythm: [[../course-rhythm]]

## Goals

- Present personal agent architecture + artistic/research use + short critical reflection  
- Close the ?group therapy? loop: hopes/fears revisited  
- Collect what should change next semester  

## Check-in (opening)

Listen only ? [[../course-rhythm#check-in-every-session]]. One sentence can answer both prompts if needed: what shifted since the outdoor first session?

## Final presentation (suggested structure)

~10?15 min + feedback:

1. **Agent architecture** ? diagram or live walkthrough (model, vault, skills, tools, phone/Discord); session 2 harness diagram updated with session 6 tools, plus `SCHEMA.md` ([[../llm-wiki-standard-files]] ? [[../what-is-harness]])  
2. **Use** ? one artistic or research scenario where the *setup* matters  
3. **Critical reflection** ? pick at least one: platform dependency, ecology, authorship, labour, regulation hype, bubble; cite at least one primary source (session [[10-authorship-labour-platforms]] / [[11-local-ecology-when-not]] tables)  
4. **Open question** you still hold  

Not a polished exhibition by default (*technical suggestion* in [[../timeline]]).

*technical suggestion:* the `log.md` of each wiki is a ready-made record of the semester ? one slide of it says more about the practice than a feature list.

## Closing round

- Return to session 1 fears/hopes ? what still stands?  
- What will you keep running after the course?  
- Feedback for the seminar format (check-in, twin tracks, agent-as-interface, student inputs)
