# Project work

This folder explains the documents for your own practice. The files are numbered in the order you meet them. The blank forms are in [templates](../templates/README.md).

The documents do not require one agent architecture. A student who works with Hermes, Cursor, a local script, a visual tool, or no AI can use the same framework.

## Sequence

| Step | Document | Content | Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Minimum path](01_minimum-path.md) | The required work. Read it first | [Manifesto](../templates/manifesto.md), [group input](../templates/group-input-presentation.md) |
| 2 | [Project path](02_project-path.md) | Manifesto, proposal, midpoint and final in one table | [Proposal](../templates/project-proposal.md), [midpoint](../templates/midpoint-check.md), [final](../templates/final-presentation.md) |
| 3 | [Provenance and process log](03_provenance.md) | The record of task, model, tools, decisions and outcome, with the running log | [Provenance note and log](../templates/provenance-note-and-log.md) |
| 4 | [Verification](04_verification.md) | How you check the result | [Verification plan](../templates/verification-plan.md) |
| 5 | [Interface score](05_interface-score.md) | Scaffold for Session 06 and later. Optional | [Interface score](../templates/interface-score.md) |
| 6 | [Reflection](06_reflection.md) | The critical reflection, with prompts and reading | [Critical reflection](../templates/critical-reflection.md) |

Steps 3 and 4 start at the midpoint and continue until the final. Step 6 is due at the end.

Related: [Verified coding](../PRACTICE/08-verification-and-provenance/local-llm-coding-with-hermes.md) and the [AGENTS.md template](../PRACTICE/01-harness-and-agents/agents-md-template.md).
