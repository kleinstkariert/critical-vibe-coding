# Project path: proposal, midpoint, final

This page follows your own project from the first plan to the final presentation. It replaces two earlier pages, a proposal page and a final page.

The required work is in [minimum path](01_minimum-path.md). This page only says when each part appears and what to show. It does not require one agent architecture. A student who works with Hermes, Cursor, a local script, a visual tool, or no AI can use it.

## The path

| Stage | Session | What you bring | Notes you keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manifesto | [01 welcome and landscape](../SESSIONS/01-welcome-and-landscape.md), then every session until the end | A short text, developed through the course. You hand it in at the end | [Manifesto template](../templates/manifesto.md) |
| Proposal (semester plan) | [04 project presentations and plans](../SESSIONS/04-project-presentations-and-plans.md) | The project, and how the agent setup enters it | [Project proposal template](../templates/project-proposal.md), draft [provenance](03_provenance.md) |

## Proposal

Write one page for Session 04 with the [project proposal template](../templates/project-proposal.md). Cover these points.

1. **Project.** What you make, and why it matters to your practice. At best this work is part of your own art project.
2. **Where the agent enters.** How *agent as interface* appears in the project. "AI will generate my visuals" is not enough.
3. **The system.** Name the seven parts from [minimum path](01_minimum-path.md): knowledge store, access boundary, model endpoint, tool set, verification method, provenance, critical reflection. A small choice is enough for each.
4. **No AI.** If you do not want to use AI, present your research or your investigative plan instead. That is a valid position.

Session 04 time per person is about 5 to 8 minutes with feedback.

## Midpoint

Show the setup as it stands, with the [midpoint check template](../templates/midpoint-check.md):

- the folder or vault layout, and the access boundary
- the model, the harness, and the tools
- a first draft of the provenance note
- one risk and one question that you want peers to answer

## Final

The final presentation in Session 08 has five parts. Use the [final presentation template](../templates/final-presentation.md). It is not a polished exhibition by default.

1. **Architecture.** A diagram or a live walkthrough of the system.
2. **Use.** One artistic or research scenario where the setup matters.
3. **Critical reflection.** What the system did to the work. See [reflection](06_reflection.md).
4. **Provenance note.** Which model, which tools, who decided, where it ran. See [provenance](03_provenance.md).
5. **Open question** that you still hold.

Students who chose research instead of AI use present their investigation.
