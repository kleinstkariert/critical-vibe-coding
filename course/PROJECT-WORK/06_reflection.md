# Critical reflection

[Minimum path](01_minimum-path.md) asks for one short text on what the system did to the work. You present it in Session 08, see [project path](02_project-path.md).

The text is in your own words. It names no required product. It can say that a tool was a bad fit, or that you chose not to use AI for a task. Doubt and refusal are valid positions.

## Questions to start from

Pick the questions that matter for your project. You do not need to answer all of them.

- What did the system make faster, and what did it make worse or flatter?
- Who else did work so that the result could exist? Think of labour, data, and platforms.
- Where did the work run, and what did that use? Think of energy, water, and money.
- Whose voice does the result sound like? Is yours still in it?
- What did you give to a platform, and what do its terms allow?
- What did you refuse, or what will you refuse next time?
- What would you do differently without the agent?
