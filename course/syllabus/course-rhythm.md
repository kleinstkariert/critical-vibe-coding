# Course rhythm

*requirement* from the brief + teaching decisions of 2026-10-27 and 2026-11-09 (eight sessions). Parent: [[timeline]] · [[course-outline]].

**Slot:** **120 minutes**, **10:00 to 12:00**, with a **5 to 10 minute break**. Session files list **clock times** (not only durations).

Teacher-only prep (not in this public repo): Obsidian vault `Teaching/CriticalVibeCoding/`.

---

## 1. Check-in (every session)

**Listen only.** No fixing during the round.

| Prompt | |
|---|---|
| 1 | How was your morning? |
| 2 | What did you do with AI since we last met, and how was that experience? |

- **30 to 60 seconds per person** → about 20 students ≈ **10:00 to 10:20**
- Visible timer; teacher models brevity
- Themes on the board *after* the round if needed

---

## 2. Theory versus practice (*requirement*, owner definition)

| | **Student input (~15 min)** | **Teacher block after that** |
|---|---|---|
| Call it | **Theory** | **Practice** (mostly) |
| Means | Humanities / social / political-economy questions (economics included): ecology, data-centre **communities**, bias, flattening, power, danger, privacy, labour, regulation hype, visions and fears | Technical teaching: harness, models/install, hardware how-to, wiki, MCP, stacks... |

**Not theory:** "What is a harness?", "How do I install Ollama?", model shopping. Those are **practice** (teacher).

## 3. Student inputs (Sessions 02, 03, 05, 06, 07)

Topics are fixed by Sebastian (9 Nov 2026) and chosen by the groups at the end of Session 01.

- **Five input slots** for about 20 students → **groups of four** (or two pairs sharing one slot). *Flag for Sebastian:* confirm group size once enrolment is known.
- One group per class, after the check-in
- About **15 minutes** (target **10:20 to 10:35**), then discussion
- Use course research + [[references]], not only YouTube. Students who do not want to use AI can do research and investigative work for the input.

## 4. Default clock (Sessions 02, 03, 05, 06, 07)

| Clock | Block |
|---|---|
| **10:00 to 10:20** | Check-in (listen only) |
| **10:20 to 10:35** | Student group: humanities input |
| **10:35 to 10:55** | Teacher bridge + start of practice (or short critical frame if needed) |
| **10:55 to 11:05** | **Break** (5 to 10 min; use the full 10 if energy is low) |
| **11:05 to 12:00** | **Technical practice** (≥45 min; usually about 55) |

Buffer is intentional: do not pack the break. Cut teacher framing before cutting the check-in or the tech floor.

### Session 01 (exception)

No student input; no ≥45 min install lab. Rough clock in [[sessions/01-welcome-and-landscape]]: check-in/opener → lecture with Q&A after each block → homework and choice of input topics. Break when the room needs it (about 10:50).

### Sessions 04 and 08 (exception)

No student input. The check-in still runs (shorter). The rest is presentations: semester plans in 04, finals in 08.

## 5. Crowding

Eight sessions are tight. Session 02 (harness + wiki + landscape + install) and Session 05 (vault + phone + Discord + MCP) are the densest; both files name what moves to homework if time runs out. DIY hardware reading stays flipped to home.
