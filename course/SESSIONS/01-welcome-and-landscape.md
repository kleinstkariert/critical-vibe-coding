# Session 01. Welcome outdoors + political-economic lecture

This session introduces a personal manifesto: how you want to use AI in your practice. It is required, with [minimum path](../PROJECT-WORK/minimum-path.md) and the input presentations. It names no product. You develop it through the course and hand it in at the end. It is not homework for this week.
The stack named below is a *reference implementation* and a *demonstration*. Hermes is one way to build a student-designed agentic production system. Sebastian will install Hermes with the class. That install is *not required for assessment*. Claude Code, Cursor, OpenCode, a local script, or another agent are valid systems.
The input-topic choice is required.

Parent: [timeline](../syllabus/timeline.md) · Outline: [course outline](../syllabus/course-outline.md) · Rhythm: [course rhythm](../syllabus/course-rhythm.md)

**Slot:** 10:00 to 12:00 (120 min). Break about 5 to 10 min when the room needs it.

## Goals

- Open a shared space for fears, hopes and current workflows (technical course + "group therapy") -> > "we all hate AI, yet we all use AI"
- Map what students already use (agents, knowledge bases, subscriptions)
- Give a first technical + political-economic frame for LLMs and agents
- Propose the default personal stack: Hermes + Obsidian + LLM Wiki (+ individual model/plan); alternatives OK
- Groups choose their student-input topic (Sessions 02 to 07)

## Part A: Intro / opener check-in

**Standing course check-in** (also used indoors from Session 02; listen only, 30 to 60 s per person; full rules [course rhythm](../syllabus/course-rhythm.md)):

1. How was your morning?
2. What did you do with AI since we last met, and how was that experience?

For Session 01, expand into the longer prompts below (still listen-first):

### How it feels

- How do you feel about AI in your daily / artistic workflow right now?
- What are you afraid of (skill loss, sameness, surveillance, dependency, job pressure, greenwashing...)?
- What do you hope for (speed, access, a co-reader, new forms...)?

### What you already use

- Which tools are in your current workflow (editors, chatbots, node tools, CAD, fab...)?
- Are you already using **agents** (tool-using systems), or only chat windows?
- Do you keep a **personal knowledge base**? Where (notes app, folder, Notion, Obsidian...)?
- Has anyone heard of / used **Obsidian**? Git? Local models?
- Do you know the term "lint"?

### Politics, economy, labour (open conversation)

- Why do these systems exist *now*: who pays, who profits, who is sold as the user?
- What do you think LLMs do to **job markets** in art, design, code, education?
- What **political** uses worry or interest you (persuasion, bureaucracy, surveillance, activism)?
- Where do you notice hype versus something you can actually use?

### Quick inventory (can be show of hands)

- Who has coded before, and with which tools?
- Who has a paid LLM subscription (which)?
- Who has used MCP, Cursor, Claude Code, Hermes, OpenClaw, or similar?

Capture themes on paper; bring them into the lecture room.

Short Q&A after each block. High priority: **Blocks 3, 4, 5 and 6** (drivers and visions, economy, ecology, labour). Block 2 is medium. Deep dives per block are linked in the lecture notes.

1. **Scope of the class:** two tracks; the agent as interface; eight sessions, midpoint in 4, final in 8.
2. **What LLMs and agents are** (medium): model, chat product, agent, context, harness, MCP (Model Context Protocol); the agent loop; how a model writes; local or cloud, with the flowchart.
3. **Drivers, visions, fears, regulation** (high): [visions fears regulation](../THEORY/06-danger-and-risk/visions-fears-regulation.md). Law: [eu ai act and datenschutz](../THEORY/05-privacy-regulation/eu-ai-act-and-datenschutz.md).
4. **Economy and the AI bubble** (high): capex, construction, revenue gap, debt, earlier build-outs; arguments for and against a burst; Tooze and the BIS labour-share scenario; the commons as an alternative. [bubble economy](../THEORY/01-political-economy/bubble-economy.md).
5. **Ecology** (high): electricity, gas, water, neighbours. [ecology](../THEORY/03-ecology-infrastructure/ecology.md). Student input: Session 06.
6. **Labour** (high): hidden work, the IMF's 40% and its critics, creative jobs, first jobs, the Luddites. [labour](../THEORY/02-labour-authorship/labour.md).
7. **Media-art workflows:** five pressures and practices; flattening. [media art workflows](../PRACTICE/07-creative-stacks/media-art-workflows.md) · [flattening and the norm](../THEORY/04-bias-flattening/flattening-and-the-norm.md).
8. **Default setup (preview):** Obsidian, Hermes, LLM Wiki, a free-to-use hosted endpoint. [default setup](../PRACTICE/01-harness-and-agents/default-setup.md) · [howto obsidian setup and sync](../PRACTICE/02-knowledge-bases/howto-obsidian-setup-and-sync.md) · [howto hermes setup](../PRACTICE/01-harness-and-agents/howto-hermes-setup.md).
