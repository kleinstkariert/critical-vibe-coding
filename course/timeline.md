## Abstract (course goal)

Students learn to build and run their own powerful LLM-agent setups while unpacking the political and economic forces that made these tools feel inevitable. The seminar is a technical workshop and a shared space to name fears, hopes, and authorship questions — with the agent itself treated as an interface for media-art practice, not only as a means to an output.

## Twin tracks (every session)

| Track | What it serves |
|---|---|
| **Technical** | Working personal agent (Hermes + Obsidian / LLM Wiki by default); phone sync; optional Discord; optional local models |
| **Critical / “group therapy”** | Check-in, political-economic framing, authorship and labour, ecological and regulatory claims |
| **Agent as interface** | Setup, skills, knowledge base, and tool wiring as artistic/technical material |

## Opening ritual (*requirement*)

**Slot:** 10:00–12:00 (120 min) + 5–10 min break. Session files list **clock times**.

Every class: **listen-only check-in** — (1) How was your morning? (2) What did you do with AI since we last met, how was your experience? Max 30–60 s/person.

From session 2: one **pair** (~15 min) — **humanities / political-economy** questions (ecology, bias, flattening, labour…). Technical teaching is the **teacher** block after that. Full map: [[course-rhythm]].

Sessions 2–11: **≥45 minutes** technical practice after the break (session 1 exempt). Long DIY → homework.
