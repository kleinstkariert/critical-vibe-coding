# Presentation and writing style: Schaufler / Quadrature

Rules for CVC lecture decks and related talks. Full agent skill: `.cursor/skills/schaufler-presentation-style/SKILL.md` (also in personal Cursor skills).

**Reference archive:** `Nextcloud/_QUADRATURE/07_Talks/2026_SchauflerResidencyTalks`. Especially: *Kunst & Kapitalismus @ Mass of Money* (notes PDF), the Fantasie tech-flow diagram, installation photos (CREDO and others).

## In short

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| One strong image or quote per slide | Bullet walls, dashboards |
| Argument in speaker notes | Reading paragraphs off the slide |
| Artworks, infrastructure photos, sparse diagrams | Neon "AI brain" stock |
| Label *claim* versus *verified fact* | Confident unsourced numbers |
| Contradiction as method (image versus market) | Tech demo cheerleading |
| Own installation photos for practice beats | Logo salad of every vendor |

## Deck design (Session 01 onward)

- Text slides: **white text on black**. Never black text on white.
- Images appear where they carry the beat, as **one full-screen image alone**. The only text on an image slide is one small credit line at the bottom (11 pt, white): who made it, where, and whether it is a photo or a rendering. Full credit in the speaker notes and in `images/ATTRIBUTION.md`; no closing credits slide. Course charts print their source inside the image.
- Exception: a diagram that means nothing without its labels explained (the hype cycle) may share one slide with its explanation text.
- No underlines, underscores, rules under titles, or shadows.
- Scandinavian minimal: sparse, calm, readable. Plain, contextual lines rather than jargon.
- Slide titles are the block titles of the lecture file.
- Slides carry what is said to students. Instructor notes ("do not teach X here", "point to file Y") stay in the lecture notes, not on slides.

## Writing rule (hard)

- **Never put an em dash (`—`) between two spaces, and never use em-dash asides** in course materials. Prefer short sentences, commas or colons.
- Number ranges are written with "to" (for example "720 to 740").

## When drafting lecture `.md` files

Each block has: **Say**, **Slides** (table of text and image slides), **Q&A prompt**, and a link to its deep-dive note.
Example: [[lectures/01-welcome-landscape-lecture]].
